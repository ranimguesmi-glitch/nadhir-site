About Us

What is Nadhir?

Nadhir is a monitoring box designed for the transformer substations of Tunisian factories. It reads the analog dials and alarm lamps already installed, listens to the siren, and sends alerts by SMS and voice call to the people who can act: the electrician, the guard, the maintenance and plant managers.

The problem. Substations are checked rarely, often at night nobody is there, and a slow overheat goes unseen until it becomes a fire. Our vision: see with intention, so that no drift goes unnoticed.

Our Solution

Powerful Features for a Safer Industry

Real-Time Monitoring

Oil temperature, winding temperature and pressure, refreshed every few seconds with live curves and thresholds.

Dial Reading with Confidence

Each reading carries a confidence score. If a dial is unreadable, Nadhir shows “check needed”, never an invented value.

Instant Alerts

SMS to the electrician and guard. No reply in 5 minutes: voice call, then maintenance manager, then plant manager.

Detailed Analytics

Alert log with who replied, what, and how fast, plus a morning review of the night’s events.
